About Me
Welcome to my personal website!
I’m excited to share my journey at the intersection of technology, creativity, and analytical thinking. After graduating with a degree in Computer Engineering, I began my professional path as an Operations Analyst at TD, where I’ve honed my skills in data analysis, organization, and problem-solving. My experience in this fast-paced environment has strengthened my ability to interpret complex information, identify patterns, and develop efficient, data-driven solutions. I’m now eager to channel these analytical and organizational strengths toward my goal of becoming a Data Analyst. I’m passionate about uncovering insights that drive smarter decisions, improve processes, and create meaningful impact. At the same time, my background in computer engineering and design has nurtured a creative mindset — one that approaches problems from multiple angles and seeks innovative, user-focused solutions. I believe that true problem-solving lies in blending logic with imagination, and I strive to bring that balance into every challenge I take on. Outside of work, I find inspiration in music, art, and science — all of which continue to shape my curiosity and broaden my perspective. I’m motivated by opportunities to think creatively, collaborate deeply, and turn data and ideas into stories that make a difference.
